Research only. The default map and H3-MON export are unchanged. All shape guides come from the original OWID artwork. Population budgets and desired positions come from the previous trial. Automatic source identities still need review.

Each connected landmass is resized as one shape. Its internal borders must meet exact part budgets, connectivity, and existing contacts. A valid previous layout can be retained if it is within the outline bound. This avoids needless border changes. If the fixed-outline search fails, a bounded coastline adjustment can also be fitted. All such cases are reported below. The completed landmasses then move as whole pieces to keep sea gaps. Internal water in the artwork stays empty. Gaps between separate landmasses can open to the sea; they are not classified as geographic lakes.
